llms.txt Generator

Build a spec-compliant llms.txt so AI tools and agents find and retrieve the right pages of your docs. Paste a page list (or a sitemap), add an optional instructions section, and publish the result at yoursite.com/llms.txt.

Basics
Pages
Anything under a ## Optional section is marked optional (agents may skip it), per the llms.txt convention.
Instructions for AI (optional)
Adds an Instructions section. This is a popular extension (used by some large docs) beyond the base spec.

Publish the result at your site root: https://yoursite.com/llms.txt. Optionally add a matching llms-full.txt with full page text later.
